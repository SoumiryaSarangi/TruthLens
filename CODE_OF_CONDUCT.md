# Code of conduct

This project is about helping people judge what they read. Contributors are held to the same standard it is built
toward: be accurate, be fair, and be kind.

## Our standards

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Be honest. Report results as measured, including failures; do not overstate what the evidence shows.
- Be welcoming to people of every background, language and script, and to beginners.
- Assume good faith, and say what you mean plainly.

Unacceptable: harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing someone's private information, and deliberately
misleading reporting of results.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where the project is represented.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact on their GitHub profile. The maintainer will review
reports and respond in good faith, and may edit or remove contributions, or restrict participation, where this code is
breached. It is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
